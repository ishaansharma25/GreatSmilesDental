About All-on-X Implants

The All-on-X technique was developed as an ideal solution for restoring complete upper and lower arches. This approach provides patients with a fixed, non-removable dental arch secured by strategically placed dental implants, completed in a single day. It is ideal for patients with dentures or those needing a complete smile restoration.

Key Benefits

  • Full-arch restoration with only 4-6 implants per jaw
  • Effective even with significant bone loss, without grafting in most cases
  • Leave with teeth the same day and leave smiling
  • Faster recovery compared with traditional implants
  • More affordable than individual implants for full-arch restoration
